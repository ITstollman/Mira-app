Support

We answer every message, usually within two business days.

Email support@getmiratryon.com with what happened and which iPhone you're on. A screen recording helps more than anything else.

The camera is black

Mira needs camera access to show you wearing the piece. Open Settings → Mira → Camera and switch it on, then reopen the app. If it's already on, force-quit Mira and launch it again — iOS occasionally hands the camera to the last app that asked for it.

The mirror stalls or drops out

Live try-on streams video both ways, so it wants a steady connection. On a weak network the session ends itself rather than showing you something wrong. Minutes you didn't use on an interrupted session go back to your balance automatically — if the number looks wrong afterwards, tell us and we'll correct it by hand.

A product link didn't work

Mira reads the garment photo off the page you paste. Some retailers build their pages in the browser, so there is nothing in the page to read, and some block us outright. If a link fails, the fastest fix is a photo of the piece from your camera roll. Send us the link too — that's how the list of stores that work gets longer.

Minutes, plans and refunds

A look costs about three seconds. The mirror runs in real time, charged up front for the session and refunded for whatever you don't use. Minute packs never expire; a Mira Pro plan refills your minutes every month.

Manage or cancel a plan in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions — cancelling in the app alone doesn't stop billing. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com. If your balance looks wrong after a purchase, email us and we'll fix it.

Restoring a purchase on a new phone

Tap Restore on the plan screen while signed in with the same Apple Account. Minute packs are tied to the device that bought them — if a balance doesn't follow you across, write to us with both devices to hand and we'll move it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes every look, every saved fit and your sign-in, because all of that lives on the phone. The only thing we hold is a minute balance against a hashed device id — email us from the phone in question and we'll clear it.

Privacy

Last updated 27 August 2026

Mira is a try-on mirror. It works best when it knows as little about you as possible, so it's built that way: no account is required to try something on, and your face never gets written down. Here is everything, in plain terms.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your looks. Every saved look — the image and the piece it was — is stored in Mira's own folder on the device. We never receive them. They leave only if you save one to your camera roll or share it yourself.
  • Your email, if you sign in. Signing in is local today: the address is kept on the device and is not sent to our servers. Sign in with Apple's private relay works the same way.
  • Your sizes and favourites. On the device, nowhere else.

What leaves the phone

  • Camera video, while the mirror is running. Live try-on is rendered by Decart, so frames are streamed to them for as long as the session lasts. Mira does not record, keep or store that video, and neither the video nor a still of it is written to our servers.
  • Product links you paste. These go to Mira's server, which opens the page and reads the garment photo. That photo is held in memory for thirty minutes so you can try it on, then dropped.
  • A hashed device id. Your minute balance has to hang on something. The app sends iOS's per-vendor device identifier, and our server hashes it (SHA-256) the moment it arrives. What gets written down is that hash and a number. The identifier itself is never stored, and the hash can't be turned back into it.
  • Photos. Mira only ever asks to add to your library, when you save a look. It cannot read what's already there.

Why we're allowed to

Where the GDPR or similar law applies: we process camera frames and product links to perform the contract you entered into by using Mira; we keep a hashed device id and a balance on the same basis; and we use analytics on the basis of legitimate interests in knowing whether the app works. You can object to the last one by writing to us.

How long we keep it

  • Camera frames — not retained; they exist only for the duration of the session.
  • Scraped garment photos — thirty minutes.
  • Hashed device id and balance — until you ask us to clear it, or the balance has been untouched for two years.
  • Analytics — per Google's Firebase retention settings, currently up to 14 months.

Who else is involved

  • Decart renders the try-on. Camera frames pass through them.
  • Google Firebase holds the minute balances, and Firebase Analytics reports app usage — screens opened, sessions, crashes — against a Google-generated app instance id, not against your name or email.
  • Apple handles every purchase. We never see a card number.

These providers operate in the United States and elsewhere, so your data may be processed outside your country under standard contractual clauses or an equivalent transfer mechanism.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There are no advertising SDKs in Mira and no third-party trackers beyond the analytics named above. We don't build a profile of you, and we don't need to know who you are for the app to work.

Your rights and your choices

  • Turn the camera off any time in Settings → Mira → Camera. The catalogue and your saved looks still work.
  • Delete a look in the app, or delete the app to remove all of it at once.
  • Ask us to clear your minute balance, or to tell you what we hold against your device — write from the device it belongs to.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or restrict your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Ask us and we'll act within 30 days, free of charge.

Children

Mira isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we'll clear the record.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, the app will say so before the change applies. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
